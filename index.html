Working as a BDR at Databricks, surrounded by data engineers, AI researchers, and platform architects, I stopped watching from the sidelines and started leaning back in. The AI Ops Specialist role feels like the role that was always waiting for me to be ready for it.

I don't just hand people documentation and walk away; I stay until the thing clicks. That instinct — to translate, to simplify, to train, to make adoption stick — maps directly onto what this role asks for: creating playbooks and FAQs, delivering training sessions across EMEA, and ensuring that AI agents don't just get deployed but genuinely get used.

On the operational side, my year as a BDR supporting enterprise accounts — including ADNOC, DP World, and Emirates — has been a masterclass in managing moving parts with precision. I have coordinated large-scale campaigns, managed event logistics for AI Day Dubai (targeting 1,000+ attendees), built tracking systems for migration pipelines, and served as the connective tissue between Account Executives, partners like Capgemini and Fractal, and end customers. But I am also clear-eyed that this role represents a meaningful step up technically, and I welcome that. I want to be the person who can write a custom query to surface a performance gap, refine a prompt based on QA findings, and speak fluently to both the sales team and the engineering team. I am deeply motivated by what Databricks is building in EMEA — and I want to be part of operationalising it from the inside, not just selling it from the outside. I would love the opportunity to speak with you about how my background, energy, and trajectory align with what the AI Ops team needs.
